## Remaining Product Decisions

The open decisions in the parity spec remain product choices, not evidence of incomplete parity:

- Whether to support multiple Book Warehouse connection profiles now or keep one active profile.
- Whether to reuse `books`/`book_files` rows for source-backed items immediately or keep warehouse-specific tables.
- Whether completed requests should auto-add synced source-backed items to the requesting user's native library by default.

## Audit Result

No implementation gap was found in this refreshed audit pass. Full test gates, lint/typecheck, whitespace checks, and local runtime smoke with configured Book Warehouse data prove the current user-level parity scope: Book Warehouse-backed ebook, audiobook, and comic inventory behaves as native BookOrbit libraries, with normal-user read/download/request capabilities and without exposing third-party storage paths or admin/system comic ingestion endpoints.
